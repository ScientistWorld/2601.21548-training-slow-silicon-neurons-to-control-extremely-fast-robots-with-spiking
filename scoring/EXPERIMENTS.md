# Experiment Overview

## randomized_air_hockey
Tests whether a submitted controller succeeds on the central fast-puck interception setting when both initial puck position and speed vary. The experiment measures task completion and contact reliability while tracking the fixed controller-size constraint that makes the result meaningful.

## velocity_range_wide
Tests whether a submitted controller remains effective when the puck speed distribution is widened substantially. This experiment measures completion and contact reliability under the harder speed range while keeping controller size fixed.
