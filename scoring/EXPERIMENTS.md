# Experiment Overview

## randomized_air_hockey
Tests whether the reproduced system succeeds on the paper's central fast-puck interception setting when both initial puck position and speed vary. The experiment measures task completion and contact reliability while tracking the fixed controller-size constraint that makes the result meaningful.

## velocity_range_wide
Tests whether the same controller family remains effective when the puck speed distribution is widened substantially. This experiment measures completion and contact reliability under the harder speed range while keeping controller size fixed.
